04why it's harder than it looks · shortcut one

It stacks two hard problems: the transcript itself is unreliable, and even a perfect transcript leaves you placing each new line against a moving mouth by hand. Both disappear if the words never become text at all — hold that thought.

05why it's harder than it looks · the receipts

We tried the transcript route for real. Here's what it takes.

to place TTS on a mouth, "the words" aren't enough — v1 asked gemini for all of this, per line

timing· start 1.70 · end 4.38 · speed 0.86 · emotion deadpan

start 1.70 · speed 0.86 · deadpan
run 2 · same clip · temp 0 "Well, I guess that's it."
start 1.55 · speed 1.00 · neutral
temp 0 locks the words — the timing digits, speed and emotion still wobble between runs. sometimes perfect, sometimes drift — and "sometimes" doesn't ship.
"just use Whisper"? ASR nails the words, free and local — but words were never the bottleneck. Placement is: exactly WHEN each syllable lands.

STS sidesteps the whole board: the words never leave the audio, so nothing has to be transcribed, timed, or re-attached to the mouth.

08the idea — the foundation everything sits on

the foundation — re-perform, don't transcribe

A voice changer, not a translator: you sing into autotune, you don't type the lyrics. The words ride along inside the sound, so there is nothing to get wrong — and the background sound, gags included, is recovered from the clip instead of regenerated. Six mechanical steps, each one small. Here they are.

The honest limit lives here: two AI voices can sound identical — told "there are exactly two speakers," the ear model still finds one. And on broken clips the lip-sync swaps mouths mid-sentence. When ear and eye both fail, you mark it yourself — one click.

stage 6 / 6mixlocal · free · endless

Make it sit — a raw AI voice never does.

add reverb, for a sense of roomthe first instinct ✗ slap-back echo on every word — keep the voice dry
high-passremove the rumble→ cut the mud300 Hz→ boost the presence4 kHz — cutting made it dull→ compresssteady the swings→ limitcatch the peaks→ normalize — last−16 LUFS · two passes
why

a fresh AI voice has no set loudness, no tone, no place in the mix. Tone first, dynamics second, loudness dead last — and every line at one loudness, with the bed ducked under it by a measured amount.

result

a master that plays like one film. Mixing never re-bills — tweak forever.

tech

audio processing: pedalboard (Spotify's effects library) + ffmpeg EBU R128 loudnorm — the same chain any DAW gives you by hand
