01The problem

AI clips don't sound like one video.

CLIP 2
the voice changes·the loudness jumps·every cut is audible

Movies never made their sound in one take either — dialogue, footsteps and music are added afterwards. So this isn't supposed to work out of the box. That's the part we have to rebuild for AI video.

02The solution — part one

Keep one voice across every clip.

find who speaks, and when→ a new voice re-performs the lines→ separate voice from background→ mix it back together

The new voice copies how each line was said — words, timing, emotion — so nothing is transcribed. The original background is kept, just turned down under each new line.

03Learnings

It's not as easy as it sounds.

01

you can't transcribe your way through it. vision models can't reliably get the words right, even at temperature zero — so the new voice copies the audio itself

02

the edge case: two speakers who sound the same. detecting speech is automated and works — but two near-identical AI voices with broken lip-sync can't be split by ear or eye; you mark it yourself

03

the voice model re-voices everything you send it. a bare moo comes back as spoken words — real animal sounds are kept from the original

04

the old voice doesn't just subtract out. it stays audible behind the new one — even real separation leaves traces

05

a raw AI voice doesn't sit in the video. no set loudness, no room — it takes EQ, compression and levelling by ear
